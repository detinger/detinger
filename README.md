# 👋 Hi, I’m Darko Etinger

I’m an Associate Professor and Dean of the Faculty of Informatics in Pula at Juraj Dobrila University of Pula, where I also serve as Advisor to the Rector for Artificial Intelligence and Digitalization.

My work focuses on **digital transformation**, **modelling and simulation**, and **AI-supported information systems**. I connect academic research, industry collaboration, and modern informatics education to develop practical, data-driven digital solutions.

## 🔬 Research Interests

Business process management, Digital process automation, Modelling and simulation, AI-supported digital transformation, Data-driven decision systems

## 🎓 Teaching

At the Faculty of Informatics in Pula, I teach courses related to: IT Management, Modelling and Simulation, Business Process Management, Information Systems

## 🏫 Institutional Work

At FIPU, we are developing: Modern informatics study programs, Online informatics education, AI integration in teaching and research, Collaborations with industry and European research initiatives
